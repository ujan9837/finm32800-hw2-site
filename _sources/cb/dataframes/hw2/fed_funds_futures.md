# Dataframe: `hw2:fed_funds_futures` - 30-Day Fed Funds Futures (ZQ)

Daily ohlcv-1d bars from Databento's GLBX.MDP3 (CME Globex) dataset, pulled with the parent symbol ZQ.FUT, so it holds every listed ZQ contract. Columns: date, symbol, open, high, low, close, volume. A contract settles at 100 minus the average daily effective fed funds rate over its month, so 100 - close is the market's forecast of that average. Outright contracts look like ZQU6; calendar spreads like ZQU6-ZQZ6 are also included and should be dropped with fedwatch.filter_outright_contracts. Thinly traded far months may have no bar on some days.
